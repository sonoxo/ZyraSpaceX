# zyraSpacex

Real-world civil aerospace systems architecture for Zyra: launch systems research, spacecraft, sustainable aviation, ground infrastructure, maritime aerospace test/recovery platforms, agriculture and closed-loop life support, and an ontology for integrating engineering evidence.

## Scope

This repository is designed around public, civil aerospace engineering sources and NASA systems-engineering practice. It is a systems-architecture and implementation program, not a weapon-design repository.

## Core Domains

- Space launch vehicle systems architecture
- Spacecraft avionics, structures, thermal, power, communications and payload integration
- Ground systems and launch/recovery operations
- Sustainable aviation and electrified propulsion research
- Non-weaponized maritime aerospace test/recovery carrier concept
- Space agriculture, controlled-environment agriculture, water and waste loops
- Digital thread / model-based systems engineering
- Evidence-backed Zyra ontology
- NASA public-source research ingestion

## Engineering Method

The program follows NASA-style lifecycle logic:

1. Stakeholder needs and mission objectives
2. Concept of operations
3. Technical requirements
4. Functional decomposition
5. System architecture and interfaces
6. Technology maturation and trade studies
7. Preliminary design
8. Detailed design by qualified engineering teams
9. Integration and verification
10. Validation in representative environments
11. Operational readiness
12. Operations, maintenance and retirement

## Safety and Governance

Detailed weaponization, military carrier construction, explosive/propellant recipes, guidance-to-target logic, or other instructions that would enable harmful weapon construction are out of scope. Engineering artifacts should remain civil, research-oriented, safety reviewed, and compliant with applicable law and export controls.
