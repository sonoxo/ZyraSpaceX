# Maritime Aerospace Test & Recovery Carrier

## Purpose

A non-weaponized ocean-going aerospace platform for:
- launch-support experiments where legally authorized
- drone and eVTOL flight testing
- reusable spacecraft/booster recovery support
- telemetry relay
- weather/ocean sensing
- mobile mission control
- emergency response
- payload integration demonstrations

This is not a military aircraft-carrier construction blueprint.

## Functional Zones

### Flight Operations Deck
- marked VTOL/UAS operating areas
- safety perimeter
- fire and rescue staging
- weather instrumentation
- optical tracking systems

### Mission Control
- redundant communications
- telemetry ingest
- tracking displays
- flight termination/status monitoring where required by regulators
- test conductor stations
- synchronized data recording

### Integration Hangar
- payload preparation
- drone/eVTOL maintenance
- avionics checkout
- clean work zones
- battery handling area

### Recovery Systems
- cranes and handling systems sized by certified naval architects
- flotation/recovery equipment
- ROV/AUV support
- contamination-control procedures

### Power and Energy
- shore-power compatibility
- battery energy storage
- renewable auxiliary generation where practical
- protected emergency power

### Safety
- aviation fire suppression
- hazardous-material segregation
- medical facilities
- muster/evacuation planning
- maritime stability and damage-control engineering

## Certification Path

Real-world implementation requires qualified naval architecture, class-society review, flag-state compliance, aviation authority coordination, maritime safety approvals, environmental review, and mission-specific range authorization.
